# FIKER design direction

## Design read

FIKER is a premium, youth-centered nonprofit platform for children, young people, parents, educators, volunteers, partners, and community members. The visual language is editorial nonprofit with quiet authority, warm human imagery, deep green/charcoal neutrals, muted sky blue, and a restrained coral safety accent. The experience should feel trustworthy and local to Gilgit-Baltistan without leaning on mountain clichés.

## Core movement

Move visitors through a clear sequence: who FIKER is → what FIKER does → what young people in Gilgit-Baltistan are facing → how to stay safe → how to ask for help → how to participate → how FIKER creates community action.

## Principles

- Lead with dignity, agency, and practical action rather than crisis imagery.
- Make local context visible through language, place, school/community references, and youth pathways.
- Use editorial whitespace and asymmetry to create a premium, established NGO feel.
- Keep the child-protection experience calm, age-appropriate, and never graphic.
- Use only verified facts; treat missing stats, contacts, partners, and emergency information as editable placeholders.
- Treat social-media imagery as reference material until FIKER approves ownership, consent, captions, and usage.

## Palette

- Deep forest: #113B34 — trust, navigation, primary actions
- Forest dark: #0B2622 — high-contrast surfaces and footer
- Warm paper: #F6F2EA — editorial background
- Charcoal: #1E2724 — body text
- Sky: #B9D8D3 — youth/environmental accents
- Coral: #D96C52 — I Need Help and safety emphasis
- Sand: #E4D6C4 — subtle supporting surfaces

## Typography

Use a display serif stack (`Georgia`, `Iowan Old Style`, `Palatino Linotype`, serif) for large headlines. Use a readable system sans stack (`Inter`, `ui-sans-serif`, `system-ui`, sans-serif) for body, UI, forms, and child-protection content. Keep body copy at readable line lengths and large enough for mobile reading.

## Layout and motion

Use an 8px spacing rhythm, generous section padding, narrow reading columns, small-to-medium radii, quiet borders, and restrained shadow. Motion is short fade/translate reveals, gentle image movement, hover underlines, and one signature scroll-driven Breaking Chains section. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes scroll-linked effects and replaces them with the final static message.

## Brand expression

Keep the FIKER name prominent, supported by the organization descriptor “Foundation for Innovative Knowledge & Educational Reform” and the working tagline “Innovate. Educate. Transform.” Use the supplied/referenced FIKER mark where quality permits. Do not redraw or invent a competing logo identity.
